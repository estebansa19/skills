### Review

1. Run the `deep-review` skill on the target: a PR number, or the current branch.
2. On your own branch before shipping, fix the blocking findings you agree with, rerun the checks,
   and say which findings you dismissed and why.
3. On anyone else's PR, change nothing. Posting comments messages other people, so it keeps
   deep-review's approval step.

**Reply:** deep-review's report, plus what you fixed or dismissed on your own branch.
